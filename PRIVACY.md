# Privacy Policy

**Studio Cam Controls**
Last updated: November 22, 2026

Studio Cam Controls does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

## What the app does on your Mac

- **Camera:** the app shows a live preview from your webcam so you can adjust its settings. Video is displayed on screen only. It is never recorded, saved to disk, or sent anywhere.
- **Face detection:** the optional "Optimize once" feature uses Apple's Vision framework, on your Mac, to find the position of a face in the preview so it can measure brightness. It does not identify who anyone is, and no face images or face data are stored.
- **Before/after comparison:** a reference frame is kept in memory only, and is discarded when you stop the preview or quit.
- **Settings:** your saved looks and preferences are stored locally on your Mac in the app's own settings (macOS `UserDefaults`).

## Network

The app makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
